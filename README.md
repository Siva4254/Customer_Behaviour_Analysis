# Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, subscription status, category-wise sales, revenue, and customer age groups.

The objective of this project is to transform customer data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations understand customer preferences, identify valuable customer segments, and improve marketing and sales strategies.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer subscription status.
- Evaluate average purchase amount and customer review ratings.
- Compare revenue and sales across product categories.
- Understand purchasing patterns across different age groups.
- Provide actionable insights to support business decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

### 1. Customer Subscription Analysis

- Approximately 73% of customers are not subscribed.
- Only 27% of customers have a subscription.
- This indicates an opportunity to improve subscription adoption through targeted offers, loyalty benefits, and personalized campaigns.

### 2. Revenue by Product Category

- Clothing generates the highest revenue among the four displayed categories.
- Accessories ranks second in revenue.
- Footwear contributes less revenue than clothing and accessories.
- Outerwear has the lowest revenue contribution.

**Business insight:** Clothing and accessories are important revenue-generating categories. Businesses can analyze their successful products and develop targeted promotions to improve the performance of lower-revenue categories.

### 3. Sales by Product Category

- Clothing records the highest sales.
- Accessories ranks second.
- Footwear and outerwear contribute fewer sales compared with the leading categories.

**Business insight:** Businesses can prioritize inventory planning and marketing campaigns for high-performing categories while exploring ways to increase demand for lower-performing products.

### 4. Revenue by Age Group

- Senior customers generate the highest revenue.
- Middle-aged customers also contribute a significant share of revenue.
- Adult customers contribute less revenue than senior and middle-aged customers.
- Customers aged 65+ represent the lowest revenue contribution in the displayed age groups.

**Business insight:** Senior and middle-aged customers are valuable segments for revenue generation. Targeted offers based on their preferences may help strengthen customer engagement and sales.

### 5. Sales by Age Group

- Senior customers record the highest sales among the displayed age groups.
- Middle-aged customers are the second-highest sales-contributing segment.
- Adult customers generate fewer sales.
- Customers aged 65+ record the lowest sales.

**Business insight:** Understanding the preferences and purchasing habits of senior and middle-aged customers can help businesses develop more effective customer-focused marketing strategies.

### 6. Customer Reviews

- The average customer review rating is 3.75.
- This metric provides a baseline for monitoring customer satisfaction and identifying opportunities to improve the shopping experience.

**Business insight:** Businesses can examine customer feedback, product quality, delivery experience, and service performance to identify areas for improvement.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Increase subscription adoption through exclusive discounts and loyalty programs.
2. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing categories such as clothing and accessories.
3. Develop targeted marketing campaigns for senior and middle-aged customers.
4. Investigate why footwear and outerwear generate comparatively lower sales and revenue.
5. Monitor customer reviews and improve the overall shopping experience.
6. Track customer behavior over time to evaluate the effectiveness of business strategies.

## 📈 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to analyze customer behavior through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

The dashboard helps identify leading product categories, customer segments contributing the most revenue, subscription opportunities, and potential areas for business improvement.
